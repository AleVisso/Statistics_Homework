Toy ECC Scheme

  • Key Roles: Private key \(k \in [1, 8]\) (let \(k=3\)). Public key \(P = k \cdot G = 3(1,5) = (5,9)\).
  • Message Encoding: Represent plaintext as valid curve point \(M(2, 10)\).
  • Encryption Steps: Select ephemeral key \(r = 2\). Compute \(C_1 = r \cdot G = 2(1,5) = (2,10)\) and \(C_2 = M + r \cdot P = (2,10) + 2(5,9) = (1,12)\). Ciphertext is \(((2,10), (1,12))\).
  • Decryption Steps: Receiver computes \(M = C_2 - k \cdot C_1 = (1,12) - 3(2,10) = (2,10)\).

Italian Caesar Cipher

  • Alphabet Set: Traditional Italian 21-letter set: A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, Z.
  • Key Role: Shift integer \(s \in [0, 20]\) (let \(s=3\)).
  • Encryption Steps: Shift characters forward modulo 21: \(C_i = (P_i + 3) \bmod 21\). "CRITTO" becomes "EUKWWR".
  • Decryption Steps: Shift characters backward modulo 21: \(P_i = (C_i - 3) \bmod 21\). Recovers "CRITTO".

Effectiveness and Limitations Discussion

Effectiveness on Long Texts: Letter frequency distributions converge closely to theoretical Italian benchmarks (\(E \approx 11.79\%\), \(A \approx 11.74\%\), \(I \approx 11.28\%\), \(O \approx 9.83\%\), \(U \approx 3.01\%\)), yielding 100% key recovery accuracy.

Limitations on Short Texts: Statistical noise and missing high-frequency vowels cause false positives in Chi-square scoring.

Alternative Approaches: Exhaustive key search (\(21\) trials) is trivial for the Italian Caesar cipher. For complex substitution, n-gram scoring (bigrams/trigrams) outperforms single-letter frequency on short samples.

Attack Comparison: Toy ECC vs. Italian Caesar Statistical Attack

The toy ECC attack is an algebraic brute-force search over a small discrete cyclic group (\(8\) candidate scalars), verifying point equality. Conversely, the Italian Caesar statistical attack exploits linguistic redundancy and frequency structures in Italian text rather than brute-forcing numerical keys.

Infeasibility on Real secp256k1 ECC

A properly configured secp256k1 curve operates on a 256-bit key space (\(\approx 1.15 \times 10^{77}\)). Exhaustive search is physically impossible due to thermodynamic constraints (Landauer's principle), preserving the intractability of the Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithm Problem (ECDLP).

Optional Analysis: Vulnerabilities from Toy Design vs. Field Size

The primary vulnerability in the toy scheme arises entirely from artificial parameter restriction (\(n=9\)), which reduces computational security to trivial enumeration. In contrast, real-world vulnerabilities in elliptic curve cryptography typically stem from implementation flaws (e.g., nonce reuse in ECDSA leading to private key leakage) rather than weakness in the underlying secp256k1 algebraic group structure.
