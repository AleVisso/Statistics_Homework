Welford Recurrence & Statistical Sampling Inference

Exercise 1: Derivation and Implementation of Welford’s Recurrence Formulas

Welford’s algorithm provides a numerically stable, single-pass method to compute running means and sums of squares without retaining historical data points in memory.

Derivation of the Mean Recurrence Formula

Let \(\mu_{k-1}\) denote the sample mean of the first \(k-1\) observations \(\{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{k-1}\}\). When a new observation \(x_k\) arrives, the exact definition of the new mean \(\mu_k\) is:

$$ \mu_k = \frac{1}{k} \sum_{i=1}^k x_i = \frac{1}{k} \left( \sum_{i=1}^{k-1} x_i + x_k \right) $$

Recognizing that the sum of the first \(k-1\) elements equals \((k-1)\mu_{k-1}\), we substitute and expand:

$$ \mu_k = \frac{1}{k} \left( (k-1)\mu_{k-1} + x_k \right) = \frac{k-1}{k}\mu_{k-1} + \frac{x_k}{k} = \mu_{k-1} + \frac{x_k - \mu_{k-1}}{k} $$

Derivation of the Sum of Squares (SS) Recurrence Formula

Let \(M_2^{(k)} = \sum_{i=1}^k (x_i - \mu_k)^2\) represent the sum of squared deviations from the current mean. Expanding for step \(k\):

$$ M_2^{(k)} = M_2^{(k-1)} + (x_k - \mu_{k-1})(x_k - \mu_k) $$

The sample variance is subsequently derived as \(s^2 = \frac{M_2^{(k)}}{k-1}\).

Python Implementation (Core Logic in 3 Lines)

def welford_update(count, mean, M2, x):
    old_mean = mean
    mean += (x - mean) / count
    M2 += (x - old_mean) * (x - mean)
    return mean, M2

Inferential Analysis & System Scale Evaluation

1. Comparison with Theoretical Parameters

The grand average of the sampling means across all \(m\) hackers converges precisely to the true theoretical population mean \(\mu = 5.0\). Similarly, the average of the individual sample variances converges to the true population variance \(\sigma^2 = 10.0\). This confirms the mathematical property of unbiasedness for sample statistics when computed using Welford's recurrence formulas.

2. Effect of Increasing System Scale (\(n\))

As the number of systems attempted by each hacker (\(n\) parameter) increases:

  • Variability of Sampling Means: The variance of the sample means decreases proportionally as \(\frac{\sigma^2}{n}\). Individual hackers' mean scores cluster significantly tighter around the population mean \(\mu\).
  • Precision of Sampling Variances: Individual sample variances exhibit narrower dispersion around \(\sigma^2\), reflecting higher asymptotic certainty.

3. Utility in Inferential Statistics and Cybersecurity

These statistical principles enable security teams to infer overarching threat levels from limited observation sets. Combining recurrence algorithms (Welford's) with large-scale sampling guarantees computational efficiency and statistical rigor in real-time intrusion monitoring.
